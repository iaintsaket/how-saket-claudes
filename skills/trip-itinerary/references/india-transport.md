# India Transport Reference Guide

## Train Classes (IRCTC)

| Class | Code | Description | Best For |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1st AC | 1A | 4-berth private coupe, highest comfort | Luxury travellers, overnight |
| 2nd AC | 2A | 4-berth open bay, AC, curtains | Premium comfort, families |
| 3rd AC | 3A | 6-berth, AC, most popular | Budget-conscious, backpackers |
| Sleeper | SL | 6-berth, no AC, fans | Budget travellers, short hops |
| Chair Car | CC | Seated, AC (Shatabdi/Vande Bharat) | Day trips, 2–4 hr journeys |
| 2nd Sitting | 2S | Seated, no AC | Very short hops only |

**Booking tips:**
- Book 60–90 days in advance for peak season (Oct–Mar, school holidays)
- Tatkal quota opens 1 day before departure at 10 AM (50–100% surcharge)
- Tourist Quota: 10% of seats reserved for international tourists at reservation counters
- PNR tracking: SMS "PNR [number]" to 139

## Bus Operators by Region

**North India:**
- HRTC (Himachal Pradesh) — reliable hill buses
- UPSRTC (UP Roadways) — covers Agra, Varanasi, Lucknow
- Rajasthan Roadways — Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur circuits

**South India:**
- KSRTC (Karnataka) — Bengaluru hub, Mysuru, Hampi, Coorg
- TNSTC (Tamil Nadu) — Chennai, Madurai, Ooty, Kodaikanal  
- KSRTC (Kerala) — Trivandrum, Munnar, Alleppey

**West India:**
- MSRTC (Maharashtra) — Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Aurangabad
- GSRTC (Gujarat) — Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Sasan Gir

**Private Luxury Bus Operators (Sleeper/AC):**
- VRL Travels — South and West India wide network
- SRS Travels — South India
- Orange Travels — South India
- RedBus — booking platform aggregating all operators

## Flight Booking Guide

**Domestic Airlines:**
- IndiGo — largest network, most reliable
- Air India — full service, good for remote routes
- SpiceJet — budget, good deals
- Akasa Air — new, competitive pricing

**Best booking windows:**
- 6–8 weeks ahead: best prices
- Under 2 weeks: prices spike significantly
- Platforms: MakeMyTrip, Cleartrip, EaseMyTrip, Ixigo

**Key hub airports:**
- Delhi (DEL) — North India gateway
- Mumbai (BOM) — West India gateway  
- Bangalore (BLR) — South India hub
- Chennai (MAA) — South India gateway
- Kolkata (CCU) — East India gateway
- Hyderabad (HYD) — Deccan gateway
- Kochi (COK) — Kerala gateway
- Goa (GOI) — Dabolim + new Mopa airport

## Self-Drive / Car Rental

- **Zoomcar** — self-drive rental, available in 45+ cities
- **Revv** — subscription-style rental
- **Local taxis (hired)** — ₹10–14/km typically, negotiate for full day
- Full-day hire (8 hrs/80 km): ₹1,500–3,500 depending on city and car type
- Outstation hire: ₹12–18/km + driver allowance ₹300–500/day

**Road conditions by region:**
- NH highways: good to excellent
- Hill stations (Manali, Munnar, Ooty): narrow, hairpin bends — experienced driver recommended
- Rajasthan desert roads: good NHs, poor state roads
- North East: road conditions variable, permit required for some areas
