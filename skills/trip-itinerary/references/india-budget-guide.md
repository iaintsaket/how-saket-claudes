# India Budget Guide — Realistic Cost Benchmarks

All prices in INR (₹) per person per day unless stated. Benchmarks last reviewed 2025; verify current prices with search.

| Tier | Daily Budget (per person) | Accommodation | Food Style | Transport |
|------|--------------------------|---------------|------------|-----------|
| 💰 Budget | ₹1,500–3,000 | Dorms/guesthouses ₹400–1,200 | Street food + local dhabas | SL train, AC bus |
| 💰💰 Mid-range | ₹3,000–7,000 | 3-star hotels ₹1,500–4,000 | Casual restaurants | 3A train, Ola/Uber |
| 💰💰💰 Premium | ₹7,000–15,000 | 4-star hotels ₹4,000–9,000 | Good restaurants + experiences | 2A/1A train, cabs |
| 💎 Luxury | ₹15,000+ | Heritage hotels/5-stars ₹9,000+ | Fine dining, curated experiences | Private car, flights |

## Hidden Costs to Flag

- **Camera fees** at many monuments: ₹25–500 extra
- **Guide charges**: ₹300–1,000 for 2–3 hrs at most major sites
- **Rickshaw/taxi tip**: ₹20–50 standard
- **Temple prasad/offerings**: ₹20–200 at major temples
- **Luggage storage** at stations: ₹30–50/bag/day
- **SIM card** (tourist): ₹300–500 for 28-day data pack (Airtel/Jio recommended)
- **Travel insurance**: Strongly recommended — ₹200–800/day depending on coverage
- **ATM fees** (foreign cards): ₹200–500 per transaction + bank fee
