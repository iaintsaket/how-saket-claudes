# India Regions & Travel Circuits

## Golden Triangle (Most Popular First-Timer Circuit)
**Cities:** Delhi → Agra → Jaipur  
**Ideal Duration:** 5–7 days  
**Best Season:** Oct–Mar  
**Key Highlights:**
- Delhi: Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, Chandni Chowk, India Gate, Lodhi Garden
- Agra: Taj Mahal (sunrise visit essential), Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri (45 min drive)
- Jaipur: Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, Nahargarh Fort sunset
**Watch out for:** Taj Mahal closed on Fridays; book Taj Mahal sunrise slot online

## Rajasthan Royal Circuit
**Cities:** Jaipur → Jodhpur → Jaisalmer → Udaipur (+ Pushkar optional)  
**Ideal Duration:** 10–14 days  
**Best Season:** Oct–Mar (avoid May–Jun, extreme heat)  
**Key Highlights:**
- Jodhpur: Mehrangarh Fort (best fort in India), Umaid Bhawan, blue city walk
- Jaisalmer: Golden Fort (only living fort in India), desert safari, camel ride
- Udaipur: City Palace, Lake Pichola boat ride, Jagdish Temple, Sajjangarh sunset
- Pushkar: Brahma Temple, Pushkar Lake ghats, camel fair (Nov)
**Watch out for:** Jaisalmer Fort entry ₹50 (Indian) / ₹250 (foreigner); desert nights cold in winter

## Kerala Backwaters & Highlands
**Cities:** Kochi → Munnar → Alleppey → Varkala/Kovalam  
**Ideal Duration:** 7–10 days  
**Best Season:** Oct–Feb (avoid monsoon Jul–Sep — beautiful but houseboat ops limited)  
**Key Highlights:**
- Kochi: Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets, Mattancherry, Kathakali performance
- Munnar: Tea estates, Eravikulam NP (Nilgiri Tahr), Top Station viewpoint
- Alleppey: Houseboat on backwaters (overnight mandatory), Vembanad Lake
- Varkala: Cliff-top beach, Papanasam Beach, Sivagiri Mutt
**Watch out for:** Houseboat prices vary wildly; negotiate directly or book via verified agents. Munnar roads — allow extra travel time.

## Goa Beach Circuit
**Areas:** North Goa (Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Vagator) | South Goa (Palolem, Agonda, Colva)  
**Ideal Duration:** 5–7 days  
**Best Season:** Nov–Mar (avoid May–Sep monsoon — most shacks closed)  
**Key Highlights:**
- North: Baga Beach, Saturday Night Market (Anjuna), Dudhsagar Falls (Mollem NP)
- South: Palolem crescent beach, silent noise parties, dolphin spotting
- Heritage: Old Goa basilicas, Fontainhas Latin Quarter, spice plantation tours
**Watch out for:** Scooter rental ₹300–500/day — best way to explore. Water taxis between some beaches.

## Himachal Pradesh Mountains
**Areas:** Shimla → Manali → Kasol → Dharamshala/McLeod Ganj  
**Ideal Duration:** 10–14 days  
**Best Season:** Apr–Jun (summer), Sep–Oct (post-monsoon clarity); Manali Dec–Feb for snow
**Key Highlights:**
- Shimla: Mall Road, Jakhu Temple, Toy Train from Kalka (book 3 months ahead)
- Manali: Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass (permit required), Old Manali cafes
- Kasol: Parvati Valley trekking, Kheerganga trek (12km, overnight)
- Dharamshala: Dalai Lama Temple, Bhagsu Waterfall, cricket stadium with mountain backdrop
**Watch out for:** Rohtang Pass closed mid-Nov to May and needs an NGT permit (book 2 days ahead online). Road landslides common in monsoon.

## Uttarakhand Spiritual & Adventure
**Cities:** Rishikesh → Haridwar → Mussoorie → Dehradun  
**Ideal Duration:** 5–8 days  
**Best Season:** Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov (Char Dham pilgrimage Apr–Nov)  
**Key Highlights:**
- Rishikesh: River rafting (Grade II–IV), Laxman Jhula, Beatles Ashram, yoga retreats, Ganga Aarti
- Haridwar: Har Ki Pauri Ganga Aarti (evening, unmissable), Chandi Devi Temple
- Mussoorie: Kempty Falls, Gun Hill, Mall Road, Landour village
**Watch out for:** Rishikesh no-alcohol and vegetarian-only zone. Rafting season Oct–May only.

## Tamil Nadu Temple Circuit
**Cities:** Chennai → Mahabalipuram → Pondicherry → Thanjavur → Madurai  
**Ideal Duration:** 7–10 days  
**Best Season:** Nov–Feb (avoid Jun–Sep monsoon)  
**Key Highlights:**
- Mahabalipuram: Shore Temple, Pancha Rathas (UNESCO), Arjuna's Penance relief
- Pondicherry: French Quarter, Auroville, Promenade Beach, ashram
- Thanjavur: Brihadeeswarar Temple (UNESCO, 1000 years old), Saraswathi Mahal library
- Madurai: Meenakshi Amman Temple (open 24hrs, busiest at evening aarti), Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace
**Watch out for:** Temple dress code strictly enforced — cover shoulders and legs. Photography restrictions inside.

## Ladakh & Spiti (High Altitude)
**Areas:** Leh → Nubra Valley → Pangong Lake → Tso Moriri / Spiti Valley (separate circuit)  
**Ideal Duration:** 10–14 days  
**Best Season:** Jun–Sep only (roads closed in winter)  
**Key Highlights:**
- Leh Palace, Thiksey Monastery, Hemis Monastery, Magnetic Hill
- Nubra: Hunder sand dunes, Bactrian camels, Diskit Monastery
- Pangong: Colour-changing lake, Chang La pass (5,360m)
- Spiti: Key Monastery, Chandratal Lake, Kibber village
**Watch out for:** Acclimatisation is CRITICAL — spend 2 full days in Leh before travelling to higher altitudes. Inner Line Permit required for Nubra/Pangong (obtainable in Leh, ₹500). No ATMs beyond Leh — carry cash.

## Northeast India (Offbeat)
**Areas:** Meghalaya → Assam → Arunachal Pradesh → Nagaland  
**Ideal Duration:** 10–14 days  
**Best Season:** Oct–Apr  
**Key Highlights:**
- Meghalaya: Living root bridges (Cherrapunji/Mawlynnong), Dawki crystal river, Shillong
- Assam: Kaziranga NP (one-horned rhino), Majuli river island, Sivasagar temples
- Arunachal: Tawang monastery, Ziro Valley, Namdapha NP
- Nagaland: Hornbill Festival (Dec 1–10, spectacular)
**Watch out for:** Inner Line Permit required for Arunachal Pradesh and some Nagaland areas. Apply online at least 2 weeks ahead. Limited ATMs in remote areas.
